         - `anomaly-scout` (provides: `detect_amount_anomalies`, exposes: `claim_amount_outliers`, score: 0.60)
       - Link all skills to expose `ask_graph` alongside their primary tool.
       - Create `[:SUCCEEDED_ON {score: float, runs: int}]` edges to `TaskType {name: "fraud_ring_investigation"}`.
